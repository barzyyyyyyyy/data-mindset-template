Start your AI assistant

  1. Open the Claude app. Click Code, then + New.
  2. Check it's yours. Above the message box: Data-mindset and your repository. If not, click the cloud chip → Cloud → Data-mindset, and + Select repository…
  3. Start. Ask: "What are we working on?" It should answer with your current lesson.
